## Mascot

Name: 朋友

Breed: Golden Labrador Puppy

Style:

- Kawaii

## Rules

- 所有页面必须使用同一只狗
- 禁止出现不同品种
- 禁止更换配色
- 禁止写实风格
- 禁止低多边形风格

## Dog Appearance

Body: Golden Yellow

Ear: Soft Golden Brown

Nose: Dark Brown

Eyes: Large Black

Blush: Light Orange

Highlights: Glossy

## Export Standard

Format: PNG

Background: Transparent

Resolution: 1024x1024

Usage:

- 512x512
- 256x256
- 128x128
